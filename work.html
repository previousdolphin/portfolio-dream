Everything here is a step toward a future vista — experiments, commissions, and long-running explorations in the craft of building for the web.

01 2024

Horizon Index

Directory / Research

A curated directory of emergent online spaces — mapping where the digital frontier is actively being written. The Horizon Index tracks micro-communities, experimental publishing ventures, and unconventional web formats that hint at where networked culture is headed. Updated quarterly, each entry is annotated with notes on what makes it worth watching.

02 2024

Vista Pages

Editorial / Long-form

A series of long-form editorial pages exploring what next-generation web experiences feel like from the inside. Each Vista Page pairs a strong photographic perspective with an essay on how digital environments are reshaping the way we see, read, and move through information. Conceived as slow-web artefacts, built to be read rather than scrolled past.

  • Writing
  • Design
  • Photography
03 Ongoing

The Lot

Archive / Ongoing

An ongoing archive of every site, landing page, and micro-experiment built in pursuit of better futures. The Lot is less a project than a practice log — raw, honest, and constantly growing. Some entries shine; many are useful failures. All of them are part of finding out where the next vista hides.

  • Archive
  • Experimentation
  • Process
04 2023

Deep Field

Interactive / Visual

An interactive visual essay that zooms from the immediate to the cosmic — using layered web technologies to convey scale, time, and the staggering distances between where we are and where things could be. Built entirely in the browser; no plugins, no apps.

  • Interactive
  • Visual
  • Web
05 2023

Margins

Publishing / Typography

A typographically obsessive web journal exploring the edges of readable design — annotations, marginalia, and the art of the footnote in the age of infinite scroll. Margins asks what happens when you treat every corner of a page as worthy of intention.
